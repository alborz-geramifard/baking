Recipe Instructions Review

Every change I recommend for the 20 recipes, one card each. Approve the ones you want and reject the rest. Nothing has been edited yet.

How to use it
  1. Tap Approve or Reject on each change. You can add a note, for example "use 1 tsp instead" or "this is intentional".
  2. Your choices are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back later.
  3. When you're done, press Copy decisions and paste the result onto the card. I'll then apply only the approved changes.
Changes marked "your call" need a number or fact only you know. If you approve one, add the answer in the note.
